Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Living Fountains Farm ("we", "us", "our") operates a digital marketplace connecting farmers in Kwale County with commercial buyers. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Contact details you provide when registering or messaging us — name, phone number, email address, business or personal ID details.
  • Transaction information — orders placed, produce supplied, and payment details relevant to fulfilling those transactions.
  • Technical information such as device and browser data, used to keep our platform secure and working correctly.

How we use your information

  • To onboard and verify buyers and sellers.
  • To process orders, deliveries and payments.
  • To communicate with you about your account, orders, or enquiries.
  • To improve our services and share relevant agronomic or market guidance with farmers.

How we protect your information

We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Access to personal data is limited to staff who need it to perform their role.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with trusted logistics, payment, or technology partners strictly as needed to deliver our services, and where required by law.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us at livingfountainsfarmke@gmail.com.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, reach us on 0114 114131 or via our contact page.
