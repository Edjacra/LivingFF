For Sellers

Why sell with Living Fountains Farm?

A steady market, fair pay, and support that helps your harvest grow.

Reach buyers you couldn't reach alone

We connect you directly to hotels, restaurants, caterers, schools and hospitals across Kwale and the coast — a market that would take years to build on your own.

Paid fairly, paid on time

We prioritise fair compensation for our farmers, with prompt payment via M-PESA as soon as your produce is sourced — no long waits, no surprises.

Advice that improves your harvest

We track buyer demand and share guidance on what to plant, when, and in what quantities — helping you plan a season with less guesswork.

Support as you grow

As demand for your produce grows, our team helps you plan land, labour and input needs so your farm can expand sustainably.

Stock managed for you

Our team coordinates pick-up schedules and storage, reducing post-harvest losses and keeping your produce moving quickly to market.

Who sells with us

Built around Kwale's smallholder farmers

Living Fountains Farm maintains consistent communication with farmers, while actively gathering and analysing data on our customers' purchasing habits. This lets us guide farmers on what to plant, when, and in what quantities — while ensuring fair, timely compensation for every harvest sourced.

Getting started as a seller

Registering is simple

In order to register online you will need:

  1. Your personal details
  2. Your Kenyan National ID number
  3. Your M-PESA mobile number
  4. Your email address (optional)

Our team verifies this information and gets you onboarded quickly, so you can start supplying with confidence.
