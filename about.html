About Us

We are a passionate team on a mission

A Kwale-based Agri-SME running a B2B digital marketplace that connects farm to fork — fairly, transparently, and consistently.

Who we are

Grown from Kwale's own springs

Living Fountains Farm takes its name from the natural springs that feed the fields of Kwale County. We are an Agri-SME offering a B2B digital marketplace designed to facilitate secure e-commerce transactions, financing and value-added services for farmers and buyers alike.

Our expertise ensures a seamless journey from farm to fork — using local knowledge and market insight to transform and organise the agricultural value chain for increased incomes, job creation and enterprise growth along the coast.

What we do

Direct access, fair prices

Through our digital marketplace, we directly connect Agri players across Kwale County with market access — offering high quality fresh produce at a fair, transparent and stable price, while connecting farmers to financial and value-added services.
