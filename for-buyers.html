For Buyers

Why buy from Living Fountains Farm?

Consistent quality, fair prices, and produce that moves fast from field to your kitchen.

Freshness you can taste

Produce is harvested, graded and dispatched within a day of ordering. Every batch passes through our Kwale packhouse for quality checks before it reaches you — no long cold-chain delays, no guesswork.

A wide, growing basket

From leafy greens and tomatoes to tropical fruit and fresh herbs, our farmer network gives you access to a broad and expanding range of produce — sourced locally in Kwale.

Ordering made simple

Place and track orders through our online marketplace in minutes. Reorder your regular basket with a single click, and adjust quantities as your kitchen's needs change.

Free, reliable delivery

We deliver directly to your address across Kwale and the wider coastal region at no extra cost, on schedules built around your kitchen's service hours.

Sourced with purpose

Buying from us supports smallholder farmers directly, fair pay for local labour, and farming practices that protect the springs Kwale depends on.

Who buys from us

Trusted across Kwale's hospitality and public sectors

Living Fountains Farm is a B2B digital marketplace serving hotels, restaurants, caterers, schools and hospitals across Kwale County. Our clients include well-known coastal businesses such as beach resorts, boutique lodges and institutional kitchens.

Getting started as a buyer

Registering is simple

In order to register online you will need:

  1. Your business' name and address
  2. Your business registration number
  3. Your Kenyan National ID number
  4. Your email address

Our team verifies this information and gets you onboarded quickly — usually within one working day.
